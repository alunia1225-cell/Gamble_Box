BLACKJACK FIXED SPEC
- Dealer: first card open, second card hidden until player turn ends.
- Player: both initial cards fully visible.
- HIT: draw one card.
- STAND: end player turn; reveal dealer hole card; dealer draws until 17+.
- DOUBLE: doubles bet, one card, auto-stand.
- SPLIT: matching starting ranks create two hands and allow sequential play.
- Blackjack pays 2.5x total return.
- Push returns the bet.
- Bust loses.
- Dealer reveal/card sounds and win/lose/jackpot/puchun effects are retained.
- Audio files remain directly in project root.
